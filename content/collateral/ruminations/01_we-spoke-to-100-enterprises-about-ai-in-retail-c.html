Insight 05

In their own words

“I worry about agent hallucination around my product range and warranty.”

“The agent does not know my pricing.”

“The agent cannot bring a human into the conversation when required.”

“The agent is unaware of my inventory in the warehouse.”

Four different concerns, one cause: the agent doesn't have the Context it needs to act. The answers already sit across ERP, DMS, SFA, dealer apps, CRM and WMS, but only 3/100 enterprises have a Context layer connecting them.

The stack exists.
Context does not.

Across 100 enterprise conversations, the constraint is rarely the absence of data. The systems already hold the facts: price, stock, credit, product, customer and transaction data. What's missing is the Context that connects them.

At Graas, we call this the Commerce Knowledge Graph: the layer that connects enterprise systems and gives AI agents the context they need to reason and act.

The next wave of enterprise AI won't be defined by models alone. It will be defined by the Context those models can access.
